What we do

Open Communications is a Marketing Consultancy that helps Clients to maximise their return on investment in Marketing Communications.

We do this by putting in place the conditions for Marketing Agencies to work better and deliver performance that is best-in-class not just nationally but globally.

Our team is made up of senior, experienced Consultants who have the expertise and know-how to support and advise clients in all of their marketing efforts. Our specific areas of expertise include:

  • Agency Selection and Pitch Management
  • Media Auditing
  • Integrated Communications Planning
We are privileged to work with Clients within a range of industry sectors, providing the specialist support they need across the full communications spectrum.
