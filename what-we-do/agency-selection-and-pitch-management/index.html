Agency Selection and Pitch Management

Open Communications are the leading Creative and Media Pitch Consultants operating in the Irish marketplace. We work with a number of Semi-State and private entrepreneurial companies, helping them to find, align and manage all their Creative and Media partners around their fundamental business and communications goals.

We support best practice across all aspects of communications management and our experience of the communications industry, at senior levels over the past 20 years allows us to confidently negotiate the most competitive commercial terms and service level agreements for and on behalf of our Clients.

Our current Client experience, along with our communications planning background gives us an unrivalled strength in assisting Agency selection, strategy development and KPI measurement.

We believe that the most successful Client and Agency relationships are long-term in nature and based on shared values and objectives. Clarity on values and behaviours filter down to the hows and whys of operating efficiencies.
